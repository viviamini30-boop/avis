Submissions

Submissions are automatically removed after 30 days. You can permanently delete an individual submission sooner.

Registered accounts

Accounts remain active until manually deleted. Use the controls on an individual account to add or subtract its displayed USD balance; this does not move money. Deleting an account does not delete its identity submission; submission details and files expire separately after 30 days.

Change administrator password

Choose a new password with 12–128 printable English keyboard characters. It is encrypted in local private storage and takes effect immediately.
